NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into and made effective as of September 30, 2026 (the "Effective Date"), by and between:

  • ABC Company, a corporation organized and existing under the laws of [State/Country], with its principal place of business at [Company Address] (hereinafter referred to as the "Disclosing Party"), and
  • John Doe, an individual residing at [Individual Address] (hereinafter referred to as the "Receiving Party").

The Disclosing Party and Receiving Party may collectively be referred to as the "Parties" or individually as a "Party."

1. Purpose

The Disclosing Party intends to share certain proprietary and sensitive information with the Receiving Party for the purpose of [Insert purpose, e.g., evaluating a potential business relationship, employment, consultancy, or transaction] (the "Permitted Purpose").

2. Definition of Confidential Information and Business Secrets

(a) "Confidential Information" shall include any non-public, proprietary, or confidential information disclosed by the Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party, whether disclosed orally, visually, in writing, electronically, or in any other tangible or intangible form. Confidential Information includes, but is not limited to: financial data, customer lists, vendor details, software code, marketing strategies, pricing structures, and internal processes.

(b) "Business Secrets" (or Trade Secrets) shall explicitly include, without limitation:

  • Proprietary algorithms, technical designs, formulas, and source code.
  • Strategic business expansion plans, unreleased product roadmaps, and competitive intelligence.
  • Supplier contract terms, margin structures, and financial projections.
  • Any information that derives independent economic value from not being generally known to the public.

3. Exclusions from Confidentiality

Confidential Information does not include information that:

  1. Is or becomes publicly known through no breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party;
  2. Was already in the rightful possession of the Receiving Party prior to disclosure, without an obligation of confidentiality;
  3. Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to or reliance on the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information; or
  4. Is rightfully received from a third party who has no duty of confidentiality to the Disclosing Party.

4. Obligations of the Receiving Party

The Receiving Party agrees to:

  • No Disclosure: Hold all Confidential Information and Business Secrets in strict confidence and not disclose, publish, reveal, or communicate such information to any third party without prior written consent from the Disclosing Party.
  • Limited Use: Use the Confidential Information and Business Secrets solely in connection with the Permitted Purpose.
  • Standard of Care: Protect the Disclosing Party’s Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own sensitive data, but in no event less than a reasonable standard of care.
  • Restricted Access: Disclose Confidential Information only to employees, contractors, or professional advisers who have a need to know the information for the Permitted Purpose and who are bound by non-disclosure terms at least as restrictive as this Agreement.

5. Term and Duration of Confidentiality

  • Effective Date: This Agreement shall take effect on September 30, 2026.
  • Duration: The Receiving Party’s duty to maintain confidentiality and refrain from disclosing Confidential Information shall continue for a period of two (2) years from the Effective Date (expiring on September 30, 2028).
  • Survival of Business Secrets: Notwithstanding the 2-year expiration, any information qualifying as a Business Secret (or Trade Secret under applicable law) shall remain strictly confidential for as long as such information remains a trade secret or business secret under applicable law.

6. Return or Destruction of Materials

Upon the written request of the Disclosing Party or upon the termination of this Agreement, the Receiving Party shall immediately return or securely destroy all tangible and electronic documents, summaries, notes, and copies containing Confidential Information and Business Secrets, certifying such destruction in writing if requested.

7. Remedies

The Receiving Party acknowledges that any unauthorized disclosure or use of Confidential Information or Business Secrets may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages alone would be inadequate. Consequently, the Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief, specific performance, and any other equitable remedies available under the law, without the requirement of posting a bond, in addition to any monetary damages.

8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of [Insert State/Country], without regard to its conflict of law principles. Any legal suit, action, or proceeding arising out of this Agreement shall be instituted exclusively in the courts located in [Insert City/County/State].

9. Miscellaneous

  • Entire Agreement: This Agreement constitutes the complete agreement between the Parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior negotiations and understandings.
  • Amendments: No modification or amendment of this Agreement shall be valid unless executed in writing by both Parties.
  • Severability: If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.
